Student data stored

The teacher dashboard may store student name, age, level, lesson progress, attendance, speaking/listening scores, stars, current goal, teacher notes, home-practice message, family code, and optional parent email.

How data is used

Records are used only to help teachers manage the course and let parents view their own child's progress with a family code.

No public student names

Public pages do not display student names, class lists, teacher notes, parent messages, or family codes.

Analytics

Analytics are anonymous counts for page visits and app installs. They do not include student names, parent emails, teacher notes, family codes, precise location, or learner records.

Deleting data

Teachers can delete a student from the dashboard. If cloud sync is connected, the app also sends a delete request for that student record.

Data minimization

Teachers and parents should avoid entering sensitive child information that is not needed for English learning progress.
